For anyone new to it: LenoreFin began as a simple Excel spreadsheet I used to manage my family's budget. But over time, I realized no existing tools gave me the control, flexibility, and privacy I wanted. So I built LenoreFin — a personal finance tracker that puts you in charge.

Designed for self-hosting, LenoreFin keeps your financial data completely local, with no third-party syncing or hidden services.

Key Features

  • Budgets - Set up budgets for things like groceries to track expenses over time.
  • Cash Flow - See where your money has been and where it's going.
  • Retirement Planning - Plan for retirement with contribution and growth projections.
  • Reminders - Set up recurring transactions as reminders for bills.
  • Account Forecasts - Predict what your account balances will look like months or years from now.
